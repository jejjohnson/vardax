Methane Single-Overpass Example

Methane source attribution from satellite XCH₄ observations is the flagship multi-instrument inversion problem. A single overpass gives observations from TROPOMI, EMIT, and (sometimes) GHGSat over a suspected emission event. The question: what was the source rate QQQ (kg h⁻¹) at the facility, and how uncertain is the answer?

This chapter walks an end-to-end methane inversion using IncrementalFourDVar (chapter 8) with multi-instrument fusion and averaging-kernel operators (chapter 11). The forward model comes from plumax (Tier I Gaussian plume); the inference is vardax.

What this exercises in vardax¶

  • IncrementalFourDVar (chapter 8): operational 4DVar with the CVT (8.6)

  • AveragingKernel (chapter 11): first-class day-one operator (D9)

  • MultiInstrumentFusion: likelihood-level composition without pre-regridding

  • GaussNewtonHessian posterior: free from the last outer iteration

  • GaussianMarkLikelihood export: hand-off to Tier V population models (D10)

  • lineax diagonal prior and block-diagonal obs cov: the per-instrument blocks are diagonal, so Σε\gmat{\Sigma}_\varepsilonΣε​ is one DiagonalLinearOperator

  • A plumax forward model as pipekit_cycle.ForwardModel (D8). The sketch’s plumax.tier1.GaussianPlume is not a plumax export; on plumax’s main the Lagrangian, Eulerian and coupled tiers call vardax themselves, as the table below shows.

  • GeoCatalog query, inference and write loop: the operational pattern. GeoCatalog is not pinned here, and vardax ships no catalogue writer.

Six-step cycle for methane¶

The example above is Step 2 (classical inference). The full cycle:

StepComponent
1 (physics)a plumax forward model (sketched above as plumax.tier1.GaussianPlume(met))
2 (classical)IncrementalFourDVar above
3 (emulator)Train an emulator, such as plumax.lagrangian.FootprintEmulator, and gate its adjoint with plumax.lagrangian.assert_emulator_calibrated
4 (emulator inference)Swap forward=plume for forward=emulator; the vardax code is unchanged
5 (amortized)AmortizedPosterior(encoder, head=ConditionalFlowHead) trained on simulated (Q,y)(Q, \vect{y})(Q,y) pairs
6 (improve)Swap any block; previous step is the oracle for validation

At Step 5, AmortizedPosterior replaces the Step 2 classes entirely, validated against Step 2’s output with the posterior gate (14.4) of chapter 14.

When to upgrade tiers¶

plumax provides four tiers of forward operator:

TierForwardWhenOn plumax’s main
IGaussian plume (analytical)Single overpass, single source, fast iterationplumax.gauss_plume fits QQQ with NumPyro and does not call vardax
IILagrangian particle transportMulti-source, sub-hour resolutionplumax.lagrangian.linear_gaussian_inversion and lognormal_inversion solve with OptimalInterpolation and LaplaceCovariance over a footprint_operator
IIIEulerian finite-volume PDELong-range transport, regional inversionplumax.les_fvm.FourDVarProblem and solve_4dvar use ThreeDVar or IncrementalFourDVar over a linear_basis
IVCoupled transport + RTML1 radiance inversion, multi-instrumentplumax.coupled.solve_coupled uses ThreeDVar with LaplaceCovariance; fuse_observations uses OptimalInterpolation with LinearObs

The vardax inference code is the same across tiers (and across Steps 2 and 4 of the cycle): only the forward slot changes. The cost of upgrading from Tier I to Tier III is in plumax, not in vardax.
